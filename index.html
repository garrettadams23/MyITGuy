FAQ

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Congress Heights, Washington, DC and help individuals and small businesses across the DC metro area. Many issues can also be handled remotely, so distance usually isn't a barrier.

What kinds of problems can you help with?

Three main areas: hands-on troubleshooting for Windows, Linux, and Android devices; software and security solutions like Microsoft 365 setup, zero-trust access, and SIEM monitoring; and Excel and ServiceNow work such as custom spreadsheets, dashboards, and reporting. Not sure which one you need? The 60-second quiz will point you in the right direction.

Do you offer remote support or only in person?

Both. Many software, security, and spreadsheet tasks can be done remotely, while hardware problems sometimes need an in-person visit around the DC area. We'll pick the fastest option when you reach out.

How much does IT help cost?

It depends on the scope of the work, so the best next step is a free consultation. Reach out through the contact form or book a time on my calendar, and I'll give you a clear estimate before any work starts.

My computer is really slow - can that be fixed?

Usually, yes. A slow computer is one of the most common issues I handle, and it's often fixable without buying new hardware - think startup cleanup, malware checks, storage and memory limits, and overdue driver or software updates.

Do you work with businesses that handle sensitive or regulated data?

Yes. With a DoD background, I'm comfortable with the access controls, monitoring, and security basics that matter for organizations handling sensitive data, and I can help you understand and prepare for frameworks like CMMC or DFARS that are common for DC-area contractors.
